About Me

A warm, conversational approach to piano study.

Welcome! I’m a retired music educator, professional pianist, and published composer who has spent a lifetime making and teaching music. After many rewarding years teaching students of all ages, I continue to enjoy helping children, teens, and adults discover the joy of playing piano.

Every student brings unique goals, interests, and experiences, and I believe lessons should be encouraging, flexible, and centered on the music that inspires you. Whether you’re beginning for the first time, developing your skills, or returning after years away, I’d be delighted to help you reach your goals.

I hold a bachelor’s degree in Music from Western Washington University and graduate work in Choral Conducting from Oklahoma State University. My decades of teaching and performing experience inform a practical, patient approach that meets you where you are and helps you grow.

Hear more: Visit my YouTube channel to see and hear some of my work.
