Meet Don

Experienced, practical, and encouraging.

I’m a retired music educator, professional pianist, and published composer who has spent a lifetime making and teaching music. I’ve worked with students of many ages and experience levels, and I believe good teaching starts with listening to what each student wants to accomplish.

I hold a bachelor’s degree in Music from Western Washington University and graduate work in Choral Conducting from Oklahoma State University. My decades of teaching and performing experience shape an approach that is patient, flexible, and grounded in real musicianship.
